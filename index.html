NextEra Energy (NYSE: NEE) Equity Research Report

Jul. – Aug. 2026

Stanton Research Group · Equity Research Summer Analyst, Energy Coverage

I chose NextEra Energy for independent coverage and built a full 3-statement model from ten years of 10-K filings. The central question was whether NextEra's premium valuation is supported by what it earns. Three independent valuation methods point to a stock trading at fair value, with any further upside depending on management delivering its guided ~8% annual earnings growth.

  • Valued NEE with a dividend discount model ($80), sum-of-the-parts ($86), and residual income model ($107), then weighed where each method was optimistic.
  • Built a bottom-up beta from 20 regulated-utility and renewable peers, unlevered and revenue-weighted, for a 0.43 beta and 6.6% cost of equity.
  • Found that return on invested capital drifts below its cost of capital around 2028, meaning new growth may consume value instead of compounding it.
  • Wrote a formal equity report with a valuation summary and presented the investment thesis.
